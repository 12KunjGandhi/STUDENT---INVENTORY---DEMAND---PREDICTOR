# Student Inventory Demand Predictor

## Project Overview
Student Inventory Demand Predictor is a web-based application designed to manage student-related inventory and predict future product demand using historical sales data.

The system helps inventory managers maintain stock records, monitor sales, identify low-stock products, and make better restocking decisions.

## Objectives
- Manage student-related inventory efficiently.
- Maintain product and category records.
- Track sales and stock transactions.
- Predict future product demand.
- Generate restocking recommendations.
- Reduce stock shortages and excess inventory.

## Key Features
- Dashboard
- Product Management
- Category Management
- Stock Management
- Sales Management
- Demand Prediction
- Restock Suggestions
- Inventory Reports
